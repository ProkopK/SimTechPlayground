# Ideas

- Ising model: simulate spins on a lattice and watch phase transitions
- Solar system: N-body gravity with orbits you can perturb
- Wind tunnel: fluid flow around obstacles
- Lorenz attractor: deterministic chaos traced in 3D
- Lennard-Jones particles: different ensembles, thermostat control and live measurement of observables
- Swarm behavior: flocking that emerges from simple local rules
- Structural mechanics: visualize stress and deformation under load
- Convection and heat transfer: rising plumes and thermal patterns
- Turbulence: watch order break down into eddies and vortices
- Electronic structure: how electrons arrange themselves in atoms and molecules
- Hydrogen wavefunctions: 3D orbital clouds you can explore
- Neural network: watch a network learn to classify in real time
- Truss builder: design your own bridge and test it to failure
